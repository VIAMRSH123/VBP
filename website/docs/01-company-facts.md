## Company story (quoted basis, from `/about-us/`)

- Established "with the intent of providing basmati and non-basmati rice of the finest quality…
  without compromising on flavor or nutritional value."
- "The visionaries behind our organization come with many years of experience and knowledge in the
  growth, cultivation, and production of rice."
- Reach, in the company's own words: "various states across India, a few countries in Africa, and
  the desert sands of the Middle East- Dubai & Qatar."
- **Mission** — "To deliver quality products and make the world experience true Indian flavors in
  its authentic form that brings joy and satisfaction to our global consumers."
- **Vision** — "To be pioneers in rice products around the world and adopt innovative strategies
  that aid in the emergence of **Bharath Gate 91** as the most trusted and preferred brand in the
  global market."
- **Quality assurance** — "we follow strict guidelines at every stage of production to maintain the
  taste and nutritious value of the product and include regulatory agencies to inspect the
  development, packaging, and distribution of the product."

## Markets served (verified)

"We distribute a wide variety of rice, spices, and pulses, for the **retail, industrial, and food
service markets**." (`/`, repeated in every footer.) Product copy additionally names **households,
hotels, restaurants, catering companies** (`/91-telangana-gold/`) and **budget cafeterias and
hotels** (`/golden-grand/`), **mid-tier restaurants, hotels, and catering companies**
(`/golden-india/`).

## Facts established from packaging artwork (readable in the pack renders)

- Rice is packed for trade, not retail shelves: **18 kg, 20 kg and 35 kg** sacks.
- Spices are packed at **1 kg**.
- Several packs are trilingual — English, Telugu and Arabic — and carry "PRODUCE OF INDIA" /
  "QUALITY PRODUCE OF INDIA".
- 91 Telangana Gold names its origin explicitly: **"TELANGANA KORUTLA"**.
- 91 Telangana Gold and 911 Sona Masoori carry a **"Satake Japan 100% Sortex Rice"** mark;
  131 is marked **"Sortex Cleaned"**.
- Spice packs carry **"100% Natural Ground Spices / No Preservatives"**.
- Packs carry the Indian vegetarian (green dot) mark.

## ⚠️ Flagged for the owner — do not publish without a decision

1. **Name inconsistency.** The website page is titled "Golden India"; the packaging says
   **"Golden Indian"**. The new site uses the packaging spelling. Confirm which is correct.
2. **"Bharath Gate" vs "Bharat Gate".** The vision statement spells it *Bharath Gate 91*; the
   product page and the pack say *Bharat Gate*. The new site uses **Bharat Gate**.
3. **Expired dates printed on a live product image.** `91paraboiledrice.png` shows
   "PKG. Date: 04/2018 / Exp. Date: 03/2020" legibly on the sack. It is still published on the
   live site. The new site uses this image at a size where the date is not legible, but it should
   be **re-shot or retouched**.
4. **Placeholder content is live.** The current homepage links to three posts titled
   "lorem-ipsum-dolor-sit…". These are visible to Google today.
5. **Mismatched modals on `/our-products/`.** "SPICES" opens a modal titled *91 Telangana Turmeric
   Powder*, "SUGAR" opens *91 Telangana Coriander Powder*, "PULSES" opens *91 Telangana Chilli
   Powder*. The categories and the modal content do not correspond.
6. **No certification is named anywhere.** The site claims quality controls and "regulatory
   agencies", but names no certificate (contrast: a competitor states ISO 22000). Nothing
   certification-shaped has been written into the new site.
7. **Two stock images on the live site carry visible agency watermarks** — see
   `03-asset-inventory.md`. They have not been reused.
8. **Mixed http/https links.** Internal navigation links point at `http://tfsuncons.com.qa/…`
   while the site is served over https.
